\chapter{Mathematical identities and admissibility}\label{app:math}
\section{Family identity}
Using Eqs.~\eqref{eq:OH} and~\eqref{eq:HO2}, the diagnostic family obeys
\begin{align}
\dot R_H={}&r_{\mathrm{H,O_2,M}}+r_{\mathrm{H,O_3}}-r_{\mathrm{O,OH}}-r_{\mathrm{OH,H_2}}
+r_{\mathrm{H,HO_2\to2OH}}\nonumber\\
&-r_{\mathrm{H,HO_2\to H_2O+O}}-r_{\mathrm{H,HO_2\to H_2+O_2}}
-2r_{\mathrm{OH,OH}}-2r_{\mathrm{OH,HO_2}}-2r_{\mathrm{HO_2,HO_2}}\nonumber\\
&+2r_{\mathrm{H_2O_2},h\nu}+r_{\mathrm{H_2O,A}}+2r_{x,\mathrm{H_2O}}+r_{x,\mathrm{H_2}}.
\label{eq:Rbudget}
\end{align}
This is an identity, not another integrated equation. The cancellation of OH+H$_2$O$_2$ follows because that event converts one OH into one HO$_2$. The O+HO$_2$ and HO$_2$+ozone events also exchange radical forms and cancel from the summed family. Self-reactions remove two members per event. These coefficients independently check the seven-species equations against the accepted family budget.

\section{Positive boundaries}
At $q=0$, every ozone loss in Eq.~\eqref{eq:O3} vanishes while association is nonnegative. At $h=0$, its association/ozone/HO$_2$ losses vanish while O+OH, OH+H$_2$, water photolysis and O($^1$D)+H$_2$ remain nonnegative sources. At $u=0$, every OH loss vanishes; at $v=0$, every HO$_2$ loss vanishes; at $w=0$, peroxide production is nonnegative. At $d=0$, all Delta losses vanish while direct/cascade production is nonnegative. At $o=0$, atomic-oxygen losses and the quadratic Barth sink vanish while its production terms remain nonnegative.

With physical background and forcing, the retained QSSA ratios are nonnegative. Their positive radiative denominator terms prevent the dark singularity that occurred for peroxide. These statements concern the continuous accepted equations, with nonnegative photolysis complements; invalid complements are rejected rather than hidden through clipping.

The pure-dark continuum with zero atoms/radicals/peroxide/Delta and arbitrary nonnegative ozone is a stationary family of the reduced network. It demonstrates why dark geometry alone cannot identify initial ozone. It is not used to define one preferred state and is not a claim of atmospheric dark equilibrium.

\section{Solar Fourier definitions}
For reproducibility, the NOAA fractional-year coefficients actually used are retained here \citep{noaa}. With day-of-year $D$, UTC hour $U$ and year length $N=365$ or 366,
\begin{equation}\gamma=\frac{2\pi}{N}\left(D-1+\frac{U-12}{24}\right).\end{equation}
The equation of time in minutes is
\begin{align}
E=229.18\bigl(&0.000075+0.001868\cos\gamma-0.032077\sin\gamma\nonumber\\
&-0.014615\cos2\gamma-0.040849\sin2\gamma\bigr).
\end{align}
Declination in radians is
\begin{align}
\delta={}&0.006918-0.399912\cos\gamma+0.070257\sin\gamma
-0.006758\cos2\gamma\nonumber\\
&+0.000907\sin2\gamma-0.002697\cos3\gamma+0.001480\sin3\gamma.
\end{align}
For east-positive longitude $\lambda$ in degrees,
\begin{equation}H_s=\left(\frac{60U+E+4\lambda}{4}-180\right)^\circ.\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:SZA} then gives geometric SZA. These explicit conventions prevent civil-timezone or sign ambiguity. The cosine roundoff guard is geometric and does not project any chemical concentration.

\section{Interpretive boundaries}
Reproducibility fixes the numerical scenario and its source identity. It does not make the prescribed profiles a validated climatology, prove a periodic attractor or certify observational agreement. Human scientific review should assess whether the declared reduced chemistry, initialized states and absent transport are adequate for the intended atmospheric application. Those judgments remain separate from whether the document compiles and its numbers match accepted evidence.
